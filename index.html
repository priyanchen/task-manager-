          Forward emails instead (preview)

          1. In Gmail open Settings, then Forwarding and POP/IMAP, and add your forwarding address. Gmail sends a confirmation code, which appears above.
          2. Create a filter (Settings, Filters and blocked addresses) for words like webinar OR invitation OR register, and choose Forward it to your address.
          3. Matching emails show up here as suggested events. Menu names can differ slightly.
